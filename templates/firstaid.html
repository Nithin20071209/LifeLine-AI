Emergency First Aid

Follow these basic steps while waiting for professional medical help. For serious or life-threatening situations, contact emergency services immediately.

Head Injury

  • Keep the person calm and still.
  • Use a cold pack wrapped in cloth for swelling.
  • Watch for worsening symptoms such as confusion, severe headache or vomiting.
  • Seek medical care if symptoms become serious.

Burns

  • Cool the burn under cool running water.
  • Do not apply toothpaste, butter or ice.
  • Cover the affected area with a clean, non-stick dressing.
  • Seek medical attention for serious burns.

Bleeding

  • Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth or dressing.
  • Keep pressure on the wound while waiting for help.
  • Use a clean bandage once bleeding is controlled.
  • Get emergency help for heavy or uncontrolled bleeding.

Snake Bite

  • Keep the person calm and as still as possible.
  • Get emergency medical help immediately.
  • Do not cut the wound.
  • Do not attempt to suck out venom.

CPR

  • Call emergency services immediately.
  • Begin CPR if the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders or use an AED if available.

Fracture

  • Keep the injured area as still as possible.
  • Do not try to straighten the injured body part.
  • Support the area while waiting for medical help.
  • Seek professional medical care promptly.

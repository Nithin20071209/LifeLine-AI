AI Care Platform

Built to make care information simpler and more accessible.

LifeLine AI is an AI-powered care assistant designed to provide accessible general guidance for health, first aid, animals, birds, plants and trees.

AI Care Assistant

Information • Guidance • Awareness

LifeLine AI brings useful care information and conversational AI together in one simple platform. It is intended to help users understand basic situations and identify when professional help may be needed.

Our Mission

Make general care and first-aid information easier to access through a simple, user-friendly AI platform.

What We Provide

General health information, basic first-aid guidance, wellness tips, nearby help information and care guidance for animals, birds and plants.

Our Goal

Create a helpful digital companion that presents useful information clearly while encouraging users to seek qualified professional assistance when needed.

Technology Stack

Technologies used to build and power the LifeLine AI platform.

Python Flask HTML CSS JavaScript Groq AI SQLite

Important Safety Information

LifeLine AI provides general educational and first-aid information. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace doctors, veterinarians or other qualified professionals. For serious or emergency situations, contact appropriate emergency services immediately.
